How to delete your data

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In the game

If the game offers it, open Profile → Delete account, confirm, and verify with the Google Play Games profile that owns the account. This deletes the matching player record, cloud save and leaderboard entries. Playing as a guest? Use Profile → Delete account → Reset guest progress to clear that device's guest progress.

Without the app

Email sleeperversestudio@gmail.com with the game name and the Play Games gamer name you used. We will verify that the account is yours before deleting it.

What is kept

Our deletion service keeps an operation record (operation ID, target player ID, hashed verification information, status and timestamps) for 30 days, then removes it. Deleting a game account does not delete your Google account, Play Games profile or Google Play purchases, and offline copies on other devices cannot be erased remotely.
