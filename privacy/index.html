Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy applies to games published by Sleeperverse Studio ("we", "us"). Each game may use only some of the services described below; the in-game screens and store listing show what a given game uses. Contact us at sleeperversestudio@gmail.com.

Information our games may use

  • On your device. Progress, settings, purchases you own, and similar game state are stored locally. You can usually play as a guest without signing in.
  • Platform sign-in. If a game offers sign-in with Google Play Games (or a similar platform service), the platform supplies a player identifier and may supply your gamer name and profile image. We do not ask for your platform password or email address.
  • Cloud saves, leaderboards and analytics. For signed-in players, our game services provider may store a player record, synced progress, scores, and gameplay events such as level starts and completions, along with app version and platform. Leaderboard entries, including your gamer name and score, can be visible to other players.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by the app store (for example Google Play). Our games check which purchases you own so they can unlock content. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Ads. Some games show optional ads through an advertising provider such as Google AdMob. The provider may process your IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics, subject to your privacy choices. Where required, the game asks for your consent first.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

We do not sell personal information, and our games do not ask for your legal name, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or files.

Services we use

Platform services (such as Google Play and Google Play Games), advertising providers, a game services provider for player records and leaderboards, and infrastructure providers that run our account-deletion tools. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Game records stay while your game account exists. You can delete your game account from inside a game that offers it, or follow the data deletion page. Deleting a game account does not delete your Google account, Play Games profile or store purchases. Copies stored offline on other devices cannot be erased remotely.

Children

Our games are intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a younger child's information was collected, contact us and we will review it.

Security and changes

Games use encrypted connections to online services. No method is perfectly secure. We will post changes here and update the date above.
